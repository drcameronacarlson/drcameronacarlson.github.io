MoBoard Bridge Trainer — Privacy Policy

Effective 23 September 2026

MoBoard Bridge Trainer does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The app saves your practice progress (problems attempted, streaks, best times), your saved scenarios, your display preferences, and the name you type when starting an assignment or exam. All of this is kept only in your device's local app storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Assignment and completion codes

Instructors can give you an assignment code, and at the end of an assignment or exam the app shows a completion code containing your name, scores and times. The app does not transmit these codes. They are only shared if you copy them and choose to send them to someone yourself, for example by email.

Network requests

To draw the 3D bridge view and its typefaces, the app downloads a graphics library from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web request, these reveal your device's IP address to those providers, subject to their own privacy policies. After the first launch these files are cached, and the app works offline. The app sends no other information over the network.

Children

The app is intended for students and adults learning maritime navigation. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or files.
